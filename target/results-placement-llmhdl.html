LiveHD resynthesis and incremental repeat

The repeat changes no RTL or options and reuses the cold run’s exact --workdir; exports and independent timing use separate outputs. CPU, memory, perf stat and export accounting are the same as for the cold run. Speedup is cold wall ÷ measured wall. OpenROAD is not repeated. An unchanged repeat restores the completed repaired netlist, placement/RC and final boundary timing together; no new IO resynthesis or repair is needed. Place time includes validation and restoration of that saved state. GP statistics on replay describe the retained placement. A missing resynthesis value means this compiler did not record that stage.

Synthesis covers recorded compiler phases before the first physical/timing stage; ABC is included in synthesis. Resynth pre runs before placement; resynth stage callbacks are nested within physical stages. Place, TD place, resize and repair include their timing callbacks. Region work is the reported per-color ABC check/remap/CEC time within those callbacks. These inclusive times must not be summed. Fired counts include diagnostics collected during the invocation; accepted and restarts record work applied here.
