Synthesis uses zero-wire timing; placement uses estimated interconnect RC with ideal clocks. No CTS or routing is run.

CPU includes waited-for descendants. RSS is the largest individual process peak; tree RSS is a 100 ms sampled process-group sum and can double-count shared pages. Measurements are serial and cold. These few cases have no headline geomean.
